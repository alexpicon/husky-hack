# Credits

The game's recordings and photos come from public sources. Their licenses require the credits below; keep this file with the folder and link to it wherever the game is shown.

## Recordings

All 20 clips (10 genuine/synthetic pairs, 4 speakers) are from the **In-the-Wild audio deepfake dataset**: N. Müller, P. Czempin, F. Dieckmann, A. Froghyar, K. Böttinger, *Does Audio Deepfake Detection Generalize?*, Interspeech 2022.
Distributed via: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SpeechAntiSpoofingBenchmarks/InTheWild
Dataset packaging license: Apache-2.0. The underlying recordings remain attributed to their original speakers and rights holders. Synthetic clips imitate the named speaker and must never be presented as authentic.
Labels (genuine / synthetic) are the dataset's own labels. The set was cut to clips whose words, transcribed, are plain and neutral.
